These 135 measurements do not all come from the same place, and the conditions behind them differ.

80 of them were measured by us on one laptop-class machine, gated for AC power, low-power mode off, nominal thermal state and verified available compute — but there is no second unit of the same SKU to cross-check against, so the two-unit test has not been run. Treat those as dev-grade figures that are honest about their own conditions, not as lab results. Laptop thermals also differ from a rack-mounted machine.

55 rows were measured on Qualcomm AI Hub's hosted phones, marked 3P throughout. Our own gating does not apply to them: their thermal state is not exposed, the run count is theirs, and their timing excludes model load and host-side framework overhead, so those rows read faster than ours by an unknown margin. See what we do and do not know about them.
